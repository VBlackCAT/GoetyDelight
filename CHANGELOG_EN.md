# GoetyDelight 1.5.3fix1 — English Changelog

## Fixes

- Fixed item count duplication when shift-clicking a **Transfer Gem** out of the **Cursed Ingot Pot** GUI.
- Fixed the multi-block orientation of the **Roast Pig** and the double loot drop caused by destroying its head and feet parts at the same time.
- Fixed non-owners being able to take servings from the **Ascension Cake**, as well as client-side ghost items.
- Fixed **Boat Stuffed Roasted Warden** losing its remaining servings when broken and placed again.
- Fixed the **Boat Stuffed Roasted Warden** structure breaking when refilled with **Nectar Jelly**.
- Fixed **DeathArrow** not disappearing after landing.
- Fixed some feast blocks not returning their container after being emptied.
- Fixed Farmer's Delight knife and tray tags failing due to reflection failures in the production environment.
- Fixed **Dolls** not being equippable in the head slot.
- Fixed the **Royal Cake** not facing the player.
- Fixed stuttering when **Lichs Chaos Stew** collides with players.
- Fixed the permanent **Strength** buff being lost when the player dies.
- Increased the compost chance for crop-related items.

## Compatibility

- Improved compatibility with older versions of **Farmer's Delight**.
- Added compatibility with **Nectar Jelly** from **Bosses' Delight**.
- Added **FTB Ultimine** compatibility: the **Apocalyptium Knife** now benefits from chain harvesting.
- Added **Right Click, Get Crops** compatibility: holding the **Apocalyptium Knife** now prioritizes the knife's bonus-drop logic.
- Added compatibility with **Sophisticated Backpacks** / **Traveler's Backpack** auto-feeding upgrades: the **Ascension Lollipop** now feeds once every 5 seconds.
- Added Goety's supreme boss-tier servants to the servant conversion blacklist.

## Additions / Changes

- Outputs from the **Cursed Ingot Pot** and **Apocalyptium Cooking Pot** no longer carry soul energy NBT.
- Dishes that require **Unholy Blood** now require the **Cursed Ingot Pot**.
- Dishes that require **Apocalyptium Ingot** or **Medal of Doom** now require the **Apocalyptium Cooking Pot**.
- The **Apocalyptium Cooking Pot** now supports **Necrotic Brazier** recipes.
